A complete Indian intraday stock selection notebook that combines live Yahoo Finance data, technical indicators, trend/momentum analysis, news sentiment, and local AI bias scoring to generate a ranked list of intraday trading candidates. It computes EMA, VWAP, RSI, ADX, MACD, Bollinger Bands, volume and support/resistance measures, applies market regime filters, and outputs a quality-filtered shortlist with position-sizing guidance for short-term trading.
